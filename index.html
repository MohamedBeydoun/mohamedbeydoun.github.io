Shopify - Credential brokerLocal and headless authentication for developers
  • Command-line tools used to fake a browser login; now credentials arrive instantly, with no pop-up and no waiting.
  • Gated each token on six checks: user credential, device certificate, device-user match, origin, posture, and identity.
  • Put package registries, cloud providers, internal APIs, and CI behind one path, so access is revoked in one place.
  • Shipped the product end to end: an on-device daemon, device enrollment, an admin interface, and a token service.
Shopify - Authorization serviceFor internally hosted applications
  • Built the front door for internal applications, verifying who you are through Okta before a request reaches the app.
  • Kept it off the request path, so an auth outage cannot take down every internal application at once.
  • Gave teams a protected front door by configuration, so nobody has to write their own login and get it wrong.
Shopify - Device-bound single sign-on sessionsRuby on Rails, Chrome
  • Led the company's first production deployment of Device-Bound Session Credentials.
  • Bound sessions to on-device hardware keys, making a stolen session cookie worthless off the device.
  • Filed three issues against the W3C specification and resolved them with Chrome's engineers.
  • Caught and fixed a replay vector before rollout, verified against hijacked sessions with the red team.
Shopify - Multi-party approvalGo, TypeScript, React
  • Made privileged production access need a second person's approval, so no one can quietly widen their own access.
  • Championed it end to end: three competing prototypes, the CISO decision review, and the interface people use.
  • Rolled out to the entire company in eight days, with breakglass procedures and graceful degradation.
Shopify - Identity, access, and internal support platformsRuby on Rails, React, GCP

Living in Barrhaven

Get to know Mohamed

I'm Mohamed, a Staff Engineer in Security Engineering at Shopify. Most of my work is about access: who someone is, which device they're on, and what they should be allowed to reach. I like making the secure way the easy way, so nobody has to pick one.

I studied computer science at McGill University after a year of biology at the University of Ottawa, and interned at Modulis VoIP and Ubisoft in Montreal before joining Shopify in 2021. These days I work remotely from Barrhaven, in Ottawa's south end.

About the neighbourhood

Barrhaven is a suburb about 17 km southwest of downtown Ottawa, home to more than 100,000 people. It was part of the City of Nepean until amalgamation in 2001, and is now split between two wards: Barrhaven West (Ward 3) and Barrhaven East (Ward 24).

The neighbourhood's white-arched Vimy Memorial Bridge, completed in 2014, crosses the Rideau River to connect Strandherd Drive in Barrhaven with Earl Armstrong Road in Riverside South.
